BookRaven

Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}. This policy covers the BookRaven service at {{SITE}}, on the web and in the BookRaven Android app.

BookRaven is a personal library: you upload your own books, read them on any device and your progress follows you. We keep what that needs, nothing more. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and your data is never sold.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address and a hash of your password (never the password itself). With Google sign-in, the name, email address and account id Google gives us.
  • Your library: the books, comics and documents you upload, their covers and details (title, author, series…), and the collections and favorites you make.
  • Your reading: where you are in each book, your reading time and statistics, highlights, notes, bookmarks, drawings on PDFs, your reading goal and your settings, so they sync between your devices.
  • What you share: share links, book clubs and their messages, books you add to the Public Library, book requests and reports you send.
  • Storage plans: if you buy one, which plan you have, its status and renewal date, and the id Stripe gives your customer record. We never see or keep your card details.
  • Devices: the app passwords you create for KOReader or other reading apps, and their names.
  • Security records: your sessions (with the device's IP address and browser or app name) and a log of security events, such as sign-ins, password changes and failed attempts, with the IP address. They protect your account and the service from abuse.

On your device, the app keeps a copy of your library and the books you downloaded so you can read offline. Signing out removes it.

Why

To run the service you asked for: store your books, sync your reading, let you share what you choose, send the emails you need (welcome, password reset, email confirmation), and keep accounts safe. We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't read your books or notes except when a book is reported as breaking the rules of the Public Library.

Who else sees it

  • People you share with: what you put in a share link, a book club or the Public Library.
  • Our hosting providers store the service's data on our behalf.
  • Our email provider delivers the emails we send you.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • Stripe processes payments for storage plans: your name, email address, payment details and billing address go to Stripe, which handles them under its own privacy policy and keeps the records the law requires for payments.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a robot when you create an account or ask for a password reset; Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details for that check.
  • Dictionary and translation: when you look up a word or translate a passage, that text (and nothing about you) is sent to Wiktionary or to the translation service (MyMemory). Chrome's built-in translator, when there is one, works on your device.

Files you upload are checked for viruses on our own server. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long

As long as your account exists. When you delete a book, it and its file are removed (the file stays only if another account has the same book). When you delete your account, everything above is deleted and a storage plan is cancelled; copies in our backups disappear within 14 days and security logs within a few weeks. Accounts that never confirm their email address are deleted after 7 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See your name and email address and change your password in Profile, and download your books at any time.
  • Delete your account and everything in it from Profile → Delete account, in the app or on the web. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by writing to us ({{CONTACT}}).

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD apply, you have these rights under them, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as hashes, and the reader opens books in a sandbox that cannot run their scripts.

Children

BookRaven is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date above changes; important changes are announced in the app.
