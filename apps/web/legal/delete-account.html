Delete your BookRaven account

For the BookRaven service at {{SITE}}, on the web and in the BookRaven Android app.

In the app or on the web

  1. Sign in to BookRaven, in the Android app or on the web (open your profile).
  2. Open Profile and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete my account…, enter your password (or, if you sign in with Google, type your email address) and confirm.

The account is deleted at once and you are signed out on every device.

Without signing in

If you can't sign in, {{CONTACT_REQUEST}} from the email address of the account, asking to delete it. We delete it within 30 days and write back when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything in the account: your name, email address and password, your books and their files, covers and details, reading progress and statistics, highlights, notes, bookmarks, drawings, collections, favorites, settings, share links, app passwords, book club memberships and messages, and the books you added to the Public Library.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A book file stays on the server only while another account has the same book in its library.
  • Copies in our backups are gone within 14 days.
  • Security logs (sign-ins, password changes and the deletion itself, with IP addresses) are kept for a few weeks, to protect the service from abuse.

See the privacy policy for everything we keep and why.
